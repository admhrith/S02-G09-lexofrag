About LexoFrag

Designed in Malaysia for Drivers Who Treat Their Cabin as Personal Space.

LexoFrag began with a straightforward observation: parked vehicles in Malaysia routinely exceed 45°C inside the cabin, causing standard alcohol-heavy air fresheners to flash off within a week or turn harsh.

Every LexoFrag vessel uses a heat-stabilized liquid fragrance base paired with natural porous wood diffusion. Whether suspended as a 10ml Classic/Deluxe diffuser or seated on the console as a 50ml Signature frosted glass bottle, each scent is calibrated to project cleanly without overwhelming the senses.
